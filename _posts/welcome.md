# Welcome

This space is dedicated to exploring ideas at the intersection of central banking, financial markets, and technology.

## What You'll Find Here

I write about:

- **Policy Analysis** - Central bank operations, monetary policy transmission, and regulatory frameworks
- **Markets Research** - Rates, collateral, fixed income, and macro dynamics
- **Technical Projects** - Building tools for research, automation, and data analysis

## My Approach

I believe in combining rigorous policy analysis with practical technical implementation. Whether it's understanding how monetary policy transmits through financial markets or building tools to automate research workflows, I'm interested in bridging theory and practice.
